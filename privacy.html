Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 August 2026

BeAccessible respects privacy and aims to process personal information lawfully, fairly and transparently in line with South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

1. Information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect contact details, organisation details, enquiry information, transaction references, service-delivery information, account information and communications with BeAccessible.

2. Why we use information

We may process information to respond to enquiries, provide products or services, manage customer relationships, administer licences or accounts, process or reconcile transactions, provide support, meet legal obligations, improve services and protect our systems.

3. Special personal information

Some accessibility or disability-related services may involve sensitive information. BeAccessible seeks to collect only what is necessary for the service. Customers should not provide medical or disability information unless it is genuinely relevant. Where an accessibility adjustment can be provided without a diagnosis, a diagnosis is not required.

4. Lawful processing

BeAccessible will process personal information on an appropriate lawful basis, which may include consent, performance of an agreement, legal obligation or a legitimate operational purpose permitted by law.

5. Sharing information

Information may be shared with service providers that support hosting, payments, communications, customer administration, analytics or secure delivery where necessary and subject to appropriate safeguards. BeAccessible does not sell personal information.

6. International services

Some technology providers may process information outside South Africa. Where cross-border processing occurs, BeAccessible will seek to use appropriate safeguards consistent with applicable law.

7. Security

BeAccessible uses reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information processed. No internet-based system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, applicable legal obligations, dispute handling and legitimate recordkeeping needs.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask whether BeAccessible holds personal information about you, request access or correction, object to certain processing, or raise a privacy concern.

10. Marketing communications

Where BeAccessible sends direct marketing, it will provide an appropriate way to opt out. Transactional and service communications may still be sent where necessary to deliver a requested product or service.

11. Cookies and analytics

If analytics or non-essential cookies are introduced, this policy and the website consent mechanism will be updated before those technologies are used where consent is required.

12. Contact

Privacy requests and concerns may be sent to hello@beaccessible.co.za.
