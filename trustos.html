    Standards applied: WCAG 2.2 Level AA baseline, with applicable Level AAA criteria and Universal Design principles used where feasible.

    Measures included: semantic regions, skip links, visible keyboard focus, strong contrast, native controls, non-colour-only module states, reduced-motion support, descriptive frame titles, and full-page alternatives.
