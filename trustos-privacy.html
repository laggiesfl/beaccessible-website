TrustOS Privacy Notice

Phase 1 consolidation demonstration

Return to TrustOS

Do not enter personal, confidential, or production information. TrustOS Phase 1 is a demonstration using sample data.

What this demonstration does

The TrustOS shell selects and displays the TrustOps Core and GrantFlow demonstrations. It does not provide a shared account service, central product database, or automated decision engine.

Information entered in the modules

Information entered during a session is used only by the prototype behaviour in the browser. The GrantFlow pilot submission is simulated and is not sent as a real application. Refreshing or resetting a module may remove the session data.

Technical records

This demonstration is hosted on Vercel. Vercel may process ordinary technical records, such as an IP address, browser details, requested pages, timestamps, and security logs, to deliver and protect the website.

Retention and processing location

BeAccessible has not added application-level storage to this demonstration. Technical records may be retained under Vercel's platform configuration and service terms, and processing may occur outside South Africa. An exact technical-log retention period and any required cross-border safeguards have not yet been established for production; qualified privacy review remains pending.

Responsible party and your choices

BeAccessible is the responsible party for this demonstration. You may ask whether BeAccessible holds information about you, request correction or deletion where applicable, or raise a privacy concern by emailing hello@beaccessible.co.za. You may also lodge a complaint with South Africa's Information Regulator.

Main privacy notice

The main BeAccessible privacy notice applies to contact made outside this demonstration.

Before production use

A production release will require documented lawful purpose, data minimisation, retention and deletion rules, access controls, security safeguards, data-subject request procedures, service-provider terms, and any required cross-border processing controls. These controls are not represented as complete in Phase 1.

Contact

For privacy questions, email hello@beaccessible.co.za. BeAccessible is based in Cape Town, South Africa.
