Find avoidable AI costs, overlapping tools and practical governance improvements through a focused, human-reviewed audit.

What the audit includes

  • Review of up to 10 AI tools.
  • Up to 3 stakeholder or user interviews, where required.
  • AI spend and licence-cost review.
  • Duplication, overlap and inactive-subscription review.
  • Accessibility and usability review.
  • Privacy, security and governance risk scan.
  • Per-tool recommendations to keep, optimise, restrict, downgrade, consolidate or retire.
  • A 30-day savings and control plan.
  • A 90-day governance and optimisation plan.
  • A human-reviewed client report delivered in accessible DOCX and PDF formats.

What you will need to provide

After verified payment, BeAccessible will send accessible intake instructions. Be ready to provide the names and purposes of the tools, licence or subscription costs, available usage evidence, relevant policies or controls, and contact details for any agreed interview participants.

Do not send passwords or payment-card details. BeAccessible does not collect card details on this website.

Delivery timing

Your human-reviewed report will be delivered within 5 working days after all required customer information and evidence have been received. The five-working-day period does not begin on the payment date unless all required information and evidence have also been received.
