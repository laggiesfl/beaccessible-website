Refund, Cancellation & Delivery Policy

Effective date: 13 August 2026

1. Delivery

BeAccessible primarily supplies digital products and professional services. Delivery may take place by secure download, email, customer account, online platform access, virtual meeting, online training, or another electronic method disclosed before purchase.

Digital products

Where immediate digital delivery is offered, access will ordinarily be made available after successful payment confirmation.

Platforms and licences

Delivery may occur through account activation, login credentials, licence activation or a secure access link. The applicable licence period and authorised-user scope will be disclosed before purchase or in the relevant agreement.

Professional services

Consulting, audits, assessments, training and customised services are delivered according to the product description, quotation, proposal, statement of work or booking confirmation.

2. Technical delivery problems

If a customer cannot access a paid digital product because of a technical issue, BeAccessible will first try to restore access, replace the file or provide an accessible alternative delivery method. If the problem cannot reasonably be corrected, an appropriate refund may be considered or provided where legally required.

3. Incorrect or duplicate payments

Verified duplicate charges, incorrect charges caused by BeAccessible or an identifiable processing error will be corrected or refunded as appropriate.

4. Change-of-mind requests

Refund eligibility depends on the type of transaction, whether access or delivery has occurred, whether work has started, any product-specific terms and applicable law. BeAccessible does not apply a blanket “no refunds” rule where statutory cancellation or refund rights apply.

5. Services that have started

Where a customer requests or agrees that a service begin promptly, cancellation consequences may differ once work has started. Where legally permissible, BeAccessible may take into account work already completed and reasonable costs already incurred.

6. Customised work

Custom reports, organisational audits, bespoke training, commissioned research, configured licences and similar work may have different cancellation conditions after custom work begins. Any non-refundable deposit or project-initiation fee will be disclosed before commitment.

7. Subscriptions and recurring services

Where a recurring product is offered, the billing period and cancellation method will be disclosed before purchase. Cancellation normally stops future renewals and does not automatically reverse charges for a period already supplied unless required by law or expressly approved.

8. How to request a cancellation or refund

Email hello@beaccessible.co.za and provide your name, product/service, payment or order reference, purchase date and reason for the request.

9. Approved refunds

Approved refunds will be processed using an appropriate method and, where supported, through the original payment provider. Bank and payment-provider processing times may apply.

10. Accessibility of delivery

If a customer encounters an accessibility barrier, BeAccessible will make reasonable efforts to provide an accessible alternative format or delivery route. A medical diagnosis is not required to request an accessible alternative.

11. Complaints

Customers are encouraged to contact BeAccessible first so the issue can be investigated. This does not prevent use of any lawful regulatory, ombud, court or dispute-resolution mechanism available.
