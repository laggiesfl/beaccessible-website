How the real no-code version works

  • Airtable stores projects, requirements, risks, tasks, meetings, and decisions.
  • Make watches for new records and sends selected data to OpenAI.
  • OpenAI returns structured analysis, summaries, and action items.
  • Make writes the outputs back into Airtable for dashboards and follow-up.
