# Phase 3 Pricing Validation — BeAccessible

**Date:** 13 August 2026

## UDL Educator Hub

The live UDL Educator Hub currently publishes:

- Free Core: R0 forever.
- Pro: R25/month or R249/year **after beta**; Pro is currently free during the public beta.
- Organisation: from R8,900/year for up to 50 educators, or R2,900/year for up to 10 educators.
- Organisation licences are currently positioned as invoiced rather than card-paid.
- Sponsor-a-School: R10,000 per school per year or R45,000 for five schools.

**Phase 3 decision:** Do not activate Payfast for the UDL Educator Hub while Pro is publicly free during beta. Revisit when beta ends or if the published commercial model intentionally changes.

## BiasLens

BiasLens remains **Available Now · Invitation Only**.

**Phase 3 decision:** No public Payfast checkout. Keep Request Consideration.

## AI Cost Audit

Prior BeAccessible productisation work defined the AI Cost Audit as a fixed-fee, repeatable professional audit with a recommended launch price of **R3,500 once-off**, subject to final approval before live charging.

The audit package includes a defined tool-cost/value review, accessibility and governance review, recommendations, 30-day actions, 90-day optimisation planning and a human-reviewed client report.

**Phase 3 decision:** AI Cost Audit is the recommended first Payfast pilot, once BeAccessible approves the final R3,500 public price and delivery turnaround.

## HR Policy Compliance Auditor

The free audit remains free. A premium rewrite/remediation offer exists conceptually, but no previously approved fixed public price was found in the current website/repository evidence.

**Phase 3 decision:** Keep the premium rewrite as Request Audit / Enquire until price, objective scope and turnaround are approved.

---

**Accessibility Compliance Note:** Commercial status is expressed in text, not colour alone. The payment pilot must use plain language, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, accessible errors, policy acknowledgement, tolerance for slower motor interaction and WCAG 2.2 AA as a minimum.
