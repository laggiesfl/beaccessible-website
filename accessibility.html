Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 13 August 2026

Our commitment

BeAccessible is committed to making its website, digital products and customer journeys usable by disabled people and people using a wide range of devices, input methods and assistive technologies.

Accessibility target

This website targets WCAG 2.2 Level AA as a minimum baseline and applies Level AAA success criteria where reasonably achievable. Universal Design principles are applied throughout design and delivery.

Measures included

  • Semantic headings and landmarks
  • Skip-to-content links
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation and controls
  • Visible keyboard focus indicators
  • High-contrast text
  • Status information conveyed with text, not colour alone
  • Responsive layouts
  • Reduced-motion support
  • Meaningful link text
  • Plain-language legal and payment information

Known limitations

Some external demonstrations and third-party platforms linked from this website are separate systems with their own accessibility status. BeAccessible aims to improve or replace barriers as products progress from demonstration to commercial release.

Need an alternative?

If you cannot access information or complete an intended task, email hello@beaccessible.co.za. Tell us what you were trying to do and what format or adjustment would help. You do not need to provide a medical diagnosis.

Feedback

We welcome accessibility feedback, including barriers encountered with keyboard navigation, screen readers, zoom, voice input, captions, cognitive accessibility or other access needs.
